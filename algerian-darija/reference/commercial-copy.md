# Commercial and marketing copy

Load this file (alongside phrasebank.md and loanwords.md) when the task is business or marketing content aimed at an Algerian audience: product listings, ads, social captions, customer replies. This is a different register from casual chat or storytelling: the grammar stays Darija, but the tone is friendlier-but-professional and the code-switching is lighter and more deliberate.

**Grounding note:** sections 1 and 4 below draw on patterns observed in a large corpus of real Algerian marketplace listings (property ads scraped from an Algerian classifieds site). Sections 2, 3, and 5 are not corpus-verified and are built from the grammar/register rules already established in phrasebank.md and narrative-patterns.md, plus general judgment. Treat 2, 3, and 5 as a reasonable starting point, not a validated source, until checked against real business copy.

## 1. Sales and CTA phrases (corpus-observed patterns)

Real Algerian sellers write listings mostly in French, with Arabic (MSA-leaning, not heavy Darija) appearing in maybe a third of listings, especially outside Alger/Oran. A few consistent patterns worth reusing when writing Darija-inflected commercial copy:

- Contact CTAs are almost always direct and short: "contactez-nous", "N'hésitez pas à nous contacter", "pour plus d'informations contactez-nous". The Darija-inflected equivalent that keeps the same directness: "عيطولنا" (call us) or "تواصلو معانا" (get in touch with us) rather than a more formal MSA "يرجى الاتصال بنا".
- Urgency/promotion language leans French even in otherwise Arabic posts: "Prix promotionnel", "Flash", "Offre limitée", "-200 millions" style discount framing. If writing in Darija, keep the number/discount in French-numeral style and wrap it in Darija grammar: "عندنا تخفيض كبير" + the French promo word if that's the norm in the category (e.g. keeping "Promo" or "Flash" untranslated reads more native than forcing an Arabic equivalent).
- Emoji-as-bullet is the dominant list format, not dashes or numbers: 📍 for location, 🏢 for building/type, 📐 for size/spec, ✅ or ✔️ for feature checklist, 📞 for contact. This pattern held across almost every listing regardless of language. Carry this into Darija commercial copy: use emoji bullets, not "- " or "1.".
- Availability/scarcity phrasing: "Livraison immédiate", "dernière occasion", "opportunité à ne pas rater" appear often. Darija-native framing for the same idea: "فرصة ماتفوتهاش" (an opportunity, don't miss it) rather than a stiffer MSA "لا تفوت هذه الفرصة".

## 2. Brand-voice register (not corpus-verified, built from existing rules)

The middle ground between casual chat Darija and formal MSA business writing. Rules:

- Keep the core grammar markers: ma-...-ch negation, دیال/تاع possession, غادي/راح future. Dropping these to sound "more professional" is the MSA-drift failure mode the base skill already warns against.
- Drop heavy slang and filler words that are fine in chat but read as unpolished in brand copy: minimize يعني as filler, avoid stacking casual interjections (آحي، آواه) in customer-facing copy.
- Religious expressions (إن شاء الله، الحمد لله) are normal in Algerian business writing too, but used more sparingly and placed at natural points (closing a message, wishing well) rather than scattered through the copy.
- French code-switching stays, but narrows to product/category vocabulary rather than general filler words. "Livraison", "promo", "qualité", "stock" are natural; peppering in "voilà", "quoi", "en fait" the way chat Darija does reads too casual for a brand voice.
- Sentence structure stays short and paratactic (same instruction as narrative-patterns.md), which also happens to match how real listings are written: short clauses, emoji-separated, not long subordinate-clause sentences.

## 3. Customer interaction templates (not corpus-verified, illustrative only)

Before/after pairs showing the shift from stiff MSA-leaning drafts to the brand-voice register above. Use as a pattern reference, not verbatim phrases to insert.

**Order confirmation**
- Stiff: "تم تأكيد طلبكم وسيتم التواصل معكم قريباً."
- Brand voice: "الطلب ديالكم تأكد ✅ غادي نتواصلو معاكم قريب إن شاء الله."

**Delivery delay**
- Stiff: "نعتذر عن التأخير في التوصيل بسبب ظروف خارجة عن إرادتنا."
- Brand voice: "سمحولنا، كاين شوية تأخير في التوصيل، خدامين باش نوصلوها ليكم في أقرب وقت."

**Out of stock**
- Stiff: "المنتج غير متوفر حالياً، يرجى المحاولة لاحقاً."
- Brand voice: "المنتج راه ماكانش دروك، بصح غادي يرجع قريب، تابعونا باش تكونو أول من يعرف."

**Thank-you for review**
- Stiff: "شكراً لتقييمكم الإيجابي، نقدر ثقتكم."
- Brand voice: "الله يبارك فيكم على الكلام الطيب، فرحتونا بزاف 🙏"

## 4. Platform formatting notes (corpus-observed for listings, general knowledge for social)

| Platform | Register | Format habits |
|---|---|---|
| Product listing / classified ad | Mixed French/Arabic, brand-voice register | Emoji bullets per spec line, short standalone sentences, contact info and CTA at the end, price stated plainly near the top |
| Instagram caption | Brand voice, lighter | 1-3 short lines, emoji used for tone not just bullets, hashtags at the end not inline |
| WhatsApp broadcast | Closer to chat register but no slang | Very short, front-loads the offer/news in the first line since previews get cut off, one clear CTA |
| Comment reply (customer service) | Brand voice, warm | Address the person's specific point first, keep it under 2-3 sentences, close with a soft religious or goodwill phrase if the interaction was positive |

## 5. Regional-safety flag rule (not corpus-verified, policy rule)

If a word or phrase chosen for commercial copy is tagged `regional` or `rare` in loanwords.md or phrasebank.md, and the copy is meant for a national audience (not a single city/region's customer base), flag this to the user before finalizing: name the regional association and offer the neutral/common alternative instead of silently picking one. A west-coded or east-coded word in a national ad is a real cost for a business, not a stylistic nitpick.
