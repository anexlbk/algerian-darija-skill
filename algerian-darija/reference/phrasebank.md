# Darija phrasebank

Reference only, load when writing more than a couple of sentences of Darija. Organized by function, not by topic, since function words are what make or break authenticity.

## Negation pattern (ma-...-ch)

This is the single most important structural marker. Almost any verb or predicate gets wrapped:
- ما نعرفش = I don't know
- ماعنديش = I don't have
- ماكانش = there isn't
- مانيش فاهم = I don't understand ("mani" = I'm not)
- ماشي هكذا = not like that

Missing the -ch ending on negation is the #1 tell of non-native/MSA-influenced Darija.

## Everyday expressions

- صحيت — well done / thanks (versatile, used for "cheers" too)
- تبارك الله — expressing admiration
- ربي يعاون — God help (used for sympathy, "good luck," or "that's rough")
- الحمد لله — reflexive response to "how are you," not just religious
- إن شاء الله — will do / hopefully, used constantly, sometimes non-committally (like "we'll see")
- بالتوفيق — good luck
- تصبح على خير — goodnight
- صباح الخير / صباح النور — good morning exchange
- واش راك؟ / واش رايك؟ / لاباس؟ — how are you / what's your opinion / you good?
- لا باس، الحمد لله — I'm fine
- يا خويا / يا اختي — bro/sis, address term, used with strangers too
- ماعليش — never mind / it's okay
- سماحلي / سمحلي — excuse me / sorry
- هاذي هي / هكا راهي — that's it / that's how it is
- زعما — supposedly / as if (skeptical tone)
- والو — nothing at all ("ماكانش والو" = there's nothing)
- غير هكا — just like that / no reason
- خلاص — enough / done / that's it (also used as "okay, fine")
- توة / دروك — right now

## French code-switching (natural, not excessive)

Common in tech, work, admin, education, modern daily life:
- "عندي rendez-vous" (I have an appointment)
- "خدام في bureau" (works in an office)
- "portable/téléphone" for phone
- "voilà", "quoi", "franchement", "normalement", "en fait" as sentence fillers
- "بصح" itself is a nativized borrowing pattern (from "pas" logic but functions as pure Darija "but")
- Numbers and prices sometimes said in French even in an Arabic sentence

Keep the sentence's grammatical backbone in Darija. French should sit inside Darija syntax, not replace it.

## Arabizi (Latin-script) conventions, if user writes/wants Latin script

- 3 = ع (3endi = عندي)
- 7 = ح (7abibi = حبيبي)
- 9 = ق (9al = قال)
- 2 = ء/أ glottal stop (sometimes ع too in casual use)
- kh = خ, gh = غ, ch = ش
- Double letters often preserved phonetically rather than by MSA spelling rules

## Register notes

- Casual/chat: short sentences, heavy فيلر words (يعني، بصح، والله), emojis, dropped subject pronouns.
- Slightly more composed (e.g. writing a caption or explaining something): keep Darija grammar and vocabulary but tighten filler words, still avoid MSA sentence structure.
- Never flip fully into MSA vocabulary (كثيرا، الآن، لماذا، هذا) mid-response — that's the fastest way to break the illusion once it's asked for real Darija.
