## Useful expressions worth knowing (meaning, not literal translation)

- **ثبت روحك (thebbet rohek)** — focus and try to recall something ("think hard, remember")
- **آحي** — interjection of regret, resignation, or "yikes"
- **آواه** — sharp interjection of refusal/disbelief ("no way")
- **زعما** — "supposedly," used with a skeptical or sarcastic edge
- **والو** — "nothing at all"
- **ثاني** (synonyms: گانة, زادة) — "also/too"
- **قداش عندي ملي...** pattern — "it's been a while since..." (قداش = how much/how long)

Skip anything not in this list unless you have independent confidence in it — don't extrapolate slang from the dictionary's raw form, since a meaningful share of its unfiltered entries are unreliable, offensive, or about specific individuals rather than general vocabulary.
